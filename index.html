How does booking work?

Send the trip form or a WhatsApp message. We confirm availability and the price in writing before anything is booked.

When do I pay?

Nothing is charged on this site. We agree the details first; payment is arranged directly with your concierge, in cash or by bank transfer.

Can I change or cancel?

Tell us as early as possible. We will confirm the change or cancellation terms for your specific booking in writing.

Do you cover Luxor, Sharm or Hurghada?

Currently we arrange Cairo, Giza, the North Coast, and Cairo airport. Ask us before booking anything outside these areas.

Is airport meet & assist available at night?

Yes. Share your flight number and arrival time and we will confirm the meeting point before you fly.
